What AliGuard does

AliGuard checks emails and links for phishing. You can paste a link or message for a manual check, or connect a Gmail account so that AliGuard scans new emails in the background.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a salted Argon2id hash of your password (we never store the password itself). If you enable two-factor authentication, an encrypted TOTP secret and hashed recovery codes.
  • Security records: sign-in sessions (hashed session identifier, IP address, browser user-agent) and an audit log of security events such as logins and password changes.
  • Gmail connection (only if you connect Gmail): an encrypted OAuth refresh token and access token for the Gmail account, the account address, and a sync bookmark. AliGuard requests the gmail.modify permission so that it can read messages, add the AliGuard/Suspicious and AliGuard/Quarantine labels, and move a phishing email out of your Inbox. AliGuard does not delete emails and does not send email from your Gmail account.
  • Scan results: for each scanned email: sender, subject, verdict and score, the reasons for the verdict, and links in defanged form. The email body and attachments are analysed in memory and are not stored. Text you paste into the manual checker is analysed and not stored.
  • Your settings: alert preferences, thresholds, allow-list entries, and feedback you submit (such as “Not phishing”).

  • Google — Gmail API access you authorise. Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Email delivery provider — used to send verification, password-reset and alert emails to you.

  • Have I Been Pwned — when you choose a password, only the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash are sent (k-anonymity) to check whether it appears in known breaches.
  • Hosting and error monitoring — the service runs on cloud infrastructure; error reports, if enabled, exclude request bodies, cookies and query strings.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. Email content is never used to train AI models.

How long we keep data

  • Scan results: {{ retention_days }} days, then deleted automatically.
  • Audit log: {{ audit_days }} days.
  • Expired sessions and unused verification/reset tokens: removed automatically after a short grace period.
  • Everything else: until you delete it or delete your account.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time from the dashboard: stored tokens are deleted and access is revoked with Google.
  • Delete my data in Settings permanently removes your account, scan results, settings, feedback and sessions, and revokes Google access (best effort).
  • You can also revoke AliGuard in your Google Account’s security settings.
  • Backups made before a deletion may persist for up to the backup retention period set by the operator.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; Gmail and 2FA secrets are encrypted at rest; sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies; and every query is scoped to your own account. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you as required by law.

Limits of the service

AliGuard is a decision aid, not a guarantee. It can miss phishing or flag a genuine email. Quarantined emails are moved, never deleted, and can be restored from the dashboard.
